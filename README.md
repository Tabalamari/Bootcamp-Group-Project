# Bootcamp Connect

## Project Brief

Two groups of people are learning side by side: software developers and business developers. Each group has something the other needs. Our task is to build a platform that brings them together, which might spark a co-founder partnership, a collaboration, a friendship, or something more.

---

## Features & Functionality

### Core Functionality (Essential)

* **Registration and Login:** Users sign up, choose which course they're on, and manage their account.


* **User Profiles:** Photo, bio, skills, interests, and what they're looking for.


* **Search and Matching:** Find people by filters, a matching algorithm, or both, showing why two people might be a good fit.


* **Messaging:** Private conversations between users.


* **Admin Screens:** Manage categories, skills, interests, courses, and users.


* **Connection Requests:** Users can express interest, and a conversation only opens if both agree.


* **Favourites or Shortlist:** Save profiles to come back to.


* **Notifications:** Alerts for new matches, messages, and requests.


* **Safety Tools:** Block and report users, plus an admin moderation queue.



### Suggested Additional Features

* **Privacy Settings:** Control who can see your profile or contact you.


* **Events or Meetups:** Post and join pitch nights, hack days, or socials.


* **Project Ideas Board:** Business students post ideas and developers express interest (or vice versa).


* **Dashboard Stats for Admins:** Sign-ups, matches made, and active conversations.


* **Account Deletion and Data Export:** A nod to GDPR.



### Stretch Goals

* AI-assisted matching or icebreaker suggestions.


* Real-time chat.


* A mobile-friendly design.
